Model for all three: caffeine becomes a list of holds, not one global setting. A hold = a session (Workbench tab or a terminal claude that reports through the hooks) + level (Awake / Lid closed) + until (its turn ends · its tab closes · I turn it off). The Mac follows the strongest hold; when the last one goes, it sleeps as usual. Holds are stored in SQLite, so a restart shows what was holding and why. Today: one setting {mode, scope, sessionId}; picking Awake on a second session silently moves the hold off the first, and nothing lists who is holding the Mac.
